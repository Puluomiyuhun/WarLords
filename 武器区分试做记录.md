## 两个兵的区分

- 北方兽人 · 裂甲勇士（108）：长柄战镐，细长下弯镐尖配短锤头，沿用原斧兵挥击动作。移除旧腰挂石，不再添加第二把大斧。原裂甲效果、基础属性和伤害配置保持。
- 山岭巨魔 · 磐石守卫（111）：宽刃重斧，保留巨魔原身体、面孔与衣甲。改用原斧兵挥砍时间轴，移除旧石头挂件。保留站稳、抗推退、抗骑兵撞飞；没有新增破甲能力。100生命、3速度、150准备帧、1450售价、130攻击距离保持；普通挥砍的平均基础伤害按原来40/3校准。出手时机和命中高度随动作改变，实战结果不会与旧突刺版完全相同。

巨魔的斧兵图集只是动作对照模板，不加入本族商店或AI兵种表。

## 制作方法

武器由内置imagegen生成透明PNG，以原版九族人物参考板约束用色与材质。随后用原作SWF的显示列表重新渲染身体，在原武器深度插入新图，保留手掌和盾牌遮挡。没有让AI逐帧重画身体。每兵147个常规帧、11个离场帧、48个击倒帧；同步生成桌面和手机图集。商店和兵栏预览使用新图集。

- 工具：`tools/ExportNativeWeapons.java`、`tools/pack-native-weapons.py`
- 接入：`种族战役2复刻/native-weapon-content.js`
- 战镐原图：`种族战役2复刻/assets/native-weapons/orc-war-pick.png`
- 巨斧原图：`种族战役2复刻/assets/native-weapons/troll-heavy-axe.png`
- 生成帧：`种族战役2复刻/assets/weapon-orc-108-*.png`、`weapon-troll-111-*.png`，手机版本在`assets/mobile`。

## 图像生成提示词

内置imagegen，未使用CLI。共同风格参考：`assets/original-races-reference.png`。

战镐：Generate one isolated upright long-handled WAR PICK weapon sprite on a genuinely transparent background. Reference is STYLE ONLY: match northern orc worn dull steel, brown wood, dark red leather and softly shaded old Flash Warlords game art. NO character, no hands, no floor, no lettering, no extra separate objects, no outer glow. This must be immediately distinguishable from an axe: a long narrow sharply down-curved armour-piercing steel beak extending to the RIGHT at top, small compact square hammer head on LEFT, no broad blade. Long straight brown wooden shaft vertical, upper metal socket and a few dark red grip wraps lower down, a restrained metal butt cap. Functional medieval pole war pick, strong readable silhouette, simple broad shading not ornate/gemmed/chibi/pixel/modern shiny icon. Three-quarter near side view compatible with humanoid facing right. Entire weapon centered with margin, height about 3.5 times head width, head about 20% total weapon height. Shaft must be dead straight with grip on same axis. Use flat quiet transparent empty background, without any black plate or drop shadow.

巨斧（复用上一轮已生成的武器图，原提示的阵营分配已改为巨魔）：Create ONE isolated transparent PNG game equipment sprite, no character, no hands, no ground, no backdrop, no text or labels, no drop shadow. Reference image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match the old Warlords 2 Flash game's softly shaded, muted, slightly rough pre-rendered equipment, thin dark edges, realistic simple shapes, NOT chibi, not voxel/pixel art, not glossy modern RPG loot icon. Small battlefield readability takes priority: one unmistakable bold silhouette, broad material shading, few details. Side / slight three-quarter view matching characters facing right. Upright composition centered, whole item visible with a little clear transparent margin, no extra separate items. This is a production equipment layer to attach to unchanged original humanoid sprites. One heavy northern orc armor-breaking WAR AXE carried on the back. Upright thick wooden haft with dark red leather binding, unmistakable huge broad asymmetrical iron axe blade at top extending LEFT, small square hammer/pick counterweight at right, broad bevel showing worn dull steel. No ornate engraving or tiny skulls. Height about 2.7 times width. Brown/red/dull steel colors from northern orc reference. This should look like a practical brutal axe, not a pendant.

最终合成时翻转巨斧，使刃口方向吻合原挥砍，并用原握柄位置替换手持武器；不是背挂。
